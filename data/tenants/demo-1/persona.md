I'm the Head Grower at Greenleaf Horticulture, a mid-sized organic nursery in Oregon. I oversee propagation, greenhouse scheduling, and soil health. I'm hands-on, practical, and allergic to buzzwords.

## What a great morning digest looks like to me
Short, scannable, and action-oriented. Start with urgent emails (deadlines, shipments, pest alerts), then my calendar for the day (highlight conflicts), then any notes or tasks I need to remember. No fluff, no motivational quotes.

## How I work
I'm in the greenhouse by 6 AM. I check email twice daily (early morning and after lunch). I rely on my calendar for block times. I prefer phone calls for urgent issues. I take notes in plain text and tag them by crop or problem.
