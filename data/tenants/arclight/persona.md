## What a great morning digest looks like to me
A great digest answers three questions in under 90 seconds of reading:
1. **What needs me today?** Not what's interesting. What requires my sign-off, my
reply, or my judgment call before end of day.
2. **What am I about to drop?** Threads gone quiet on my end. A direct report's
workload flag I skimmed past. A customer escalation that's been sitting.
3. **What can I dispatch right now?** Short replies, quick approvals, calendar
accepts/declines — drafts I can review and send in under a minute.
A bad digest tells me about my own calendar like I haven't seen it, or treats a
recruiter cold-email as something worth my attention.
## How I work

I log off. Anything outside those windows waits.

scheduled over that block without asking are a problem I want flagged.
